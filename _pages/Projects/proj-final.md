> **Core Concepts — why this matters.** This final project ties together the essential OS topics of the course by having you study and present a working operating-system kernel from the ground up. Whichever chapter you choose — boot process, segmentation, interrupts, or process management — you will connect it back to the kernel internals, scheduling, memory, and file-system concepts you practiced all semester.

In a group of up to 3 members, choose a chapter(s) of the [539 Kernel](https://539kernel.com/A_Journey_in_Creating_an_Operating_System_Kernel_The_539kernel_Book.pdf) book and present a 20 minute presentation and demo of the features of that chapter of the book.
